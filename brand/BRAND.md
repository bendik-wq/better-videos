# MAINSPRING: brand kit

**Name:** Mainspring. **Handle:** @Mainspring (it returned "not found" on YouTube on 19 Oct 2026, so it looked free; claim it soon).
**Backups (also free that day):** @Clockwork, @Undertow, @Driveshaft.
**Tagline:** The hidden machinery behind the world.
**Why the name:** a mainspring is the coiled spring that powers a clock: hidden, small and running everything. Each
episode finds one of those: the rain behind the Panama Canal, the auction behind your mortgage rate.

Rebuild everything with `node brand/make.mjs`. The files land in `brand/out/`.

| File | Use | YouTube spec |
|---|---|---|
| `avatar-800.png` | Profile picture (lead: navy coil on paper, teal ring, red arbor) | 800×800, shown as a circle |
| `avatar-red-800.png` | Alt profile picture or socials (white coil on red) | 800×800 |
| `banner-2560x1440.png` | Channel banner. The name and tagline sit inside the 1546×423 safe area; the image cards show only on desktop and TV | 2560×1440, ≤6 MB |
| `watermark-150.png` | Video watermark (Customization → Branding → Video watermark, "entire video") | 150×150 PNG, transparent |
| `lockup.png` | Wordmark + tagline (end screens, socials, intro card) | transparent PNG |
| `logo.svg` | Vector mark | — |
| `thumb-*.jpg` | Lead thumbnails with the small coil badge top-left (away from the duration stamp) | 1280×720 |

**Palette:** paper `#f7f6f1` · ink navy `#14233d` · teal `#2a8c86` · sage `#a9c5ae` · red `#e0241b` (one red thing per frame).
**Type:** Inter 900 (wordmark and thumbnails), Inter 600 (tagline), IBM Plex Mono (data), Instrument Serif (quotes).

## Channel description (About)

> Mainspring makes bright, sourced documentaries about the hidden machinery behind the world: the auctions, canals,
> grids and money flows that quietly run everyday life. Every number on screen comes from an official source, listed
> in the description. New investigations about money, power and the systems nobody explains.

**Channel keywords:** documentary, explained, economics, finance, mortgage rates, bond market, shipping, Panama Canal,
infrastructure, geopolitics, 3D animation, data visualization

**Rules for thumbnails:** keep the episode's own hero and ≤3 words. The coil badge stays small, top-left, every time;
consistency is what builds recognition, not size.
