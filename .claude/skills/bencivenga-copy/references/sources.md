# Sources

First researched 2026-11-05 with web search and fetch; revised the same day after a critic pass
(the main change: the primary newsletter text, S16, now backs most of the method).

Bencivenga published little under his own name beyond his newsletter (*Marketing Bullets*, 29
issues, marketingbullets.com) and the *Marketing Maxims* site. His seminars (the 2006 "7 Master
Secrets of Wealth Creation" retirement seminar on DVD, and the "Bencivenga 100") circulate mainly
as student notes. So this skill labels every idea:

- **[primary]**: his own site, newsletter or interview answers;
- **[student]**: a person who attended his seminars or worked with him;
- **[secondary]**: a third-party summary;
- **[house]**: our adaptation to YouTube, not his claim (evidence status in `checklist.md` §10).

Quotes in the skill are short; everything else is paraphrased. Verify exact wording against the
primary text before quoting any of it on screen.

## Not used as evidence

- *Persuasion Equation* by Mark Rodgers (AMACOM, 2015) is a different book with a different
  formula.
- Claims about Bencivenga's hit rate (e.g. "80% of promotions beat the control") appear on blogs
  but weren't verified. Roy Furr's "80% chance of a winner" post rests on "I once heard"; don't
  repeat it.
