# Topic bank: 256 scored video ideas

Ideas in AI, tech, money, power, geopolitics and business mysteries, each with a working title,
a thumbnail concept in the house style, a one-line hook and a score. The `idea-engine` skill
draws from this file, adds fresh ideas from news searches, rescores and hands the winner to
`packaging`. **Every number in a hook is a lead to verify**, not a fact to ship: it goes through
`fact-check-playbook.md` before it appears in a script, title or thumbnail.

Written 2026-10-12. Timely ideas (T) age fast; rescore them before use.

## How each idea is scored (out of 25)

**1. Outlier potential, 0–10.** The five reasons videos explode, from our 20-channel outlier data
(`docs/research/channel-breakdowns.md` §0). Each factor scores 0 (absent), 1 (partial) or 2 (strong).
The `F` column lists the five digits in this order:

| Factor | What earns a 2 | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| F1 Absurd object + number | One concrete thing with a number on it that is the question (96,000,000 balls; a $100 trillion bill) | Veritasium 112M, PolyMatter 7.6M |
| F2 Belief reopened | The viewer already holds an opinion or half-knowledge ("Worse Than You Thought", "Why People Think") | Moon 7.9M, Johnny Harris 17M |
| F3 Timely + best explainer | A live news hook where we can be the clearest video, fast | Kurzgesagt Coronavirus 89M, Fern Trump shooting 21M |
| F4 Known villain or disaster | A name or event people have half-heard of, told completely | ColdFusion Theranos 12M, Apollo 13 17M |
| F5 Familiar frame, one twist | A known frame bent once ("Cities at Sea", "The Kids Who Hacked The CIA") | Wendover 14M, Fern 20M |

**2. Lifespan (Life), 2–5.** E = evergreen (5), H = hybrid, an evergreen core with a news peg (4),
T = timely (2). Timely ideas earn F3 instead; evergreen ideas keep earning (PolyMatter's and
Neo's biggest videos are years old and still the top of their lists [data]).

**3. Research availability (Res), 1–5.** 5 = primary records exist and are public (court records,
filings, official reports); 3 = good journalism but few primary documents; 1 = mostly rumour. The
key sources are listed per row so the research pack can start immediately.

**4. Visual potential (Vis), 1–5.** How well it builds in our engine: a hero object, a place we can
bake (`engine/world/bake.py`), a map, a document. 5 = the thumbnail and the cold open are obvious
and buildable; 3 = mostly charts and talk; 1 = abstract with no object.

**5. Saturation (Sat), 0 to −2.** −1 if a strong video or documentary on the same angle exists; −2 if
a top channel in our set already has a multi-million-view video on it (named in the row). Sat was
set from our channel lists and general knowledge on 2026-10-12; "none noted" means we didn't find a
strong competitor, not that none exists. `idea-engine` Step 4 re-checks it with a YouTube search. A
saturated topic needs a genuinely new angle or primary material to be worth making.

**Total = Out + Life + Res + Vis + Sat.** Ties are broken by lower saturation, then outlier score,
then visual potential. **★ marks the top 30.**

How to use the score: ≥22 is a candidate; 19–21 needs a sharper angle (try the frames in
`packaging` Step 1); ≤18 is filed. The score ranks ideas; it never replaces the packaging test
(25 titles and 3 different thumbnails, or kill it).
