# Moon: video autopsies

Moon is an anonymous, deep-voiced, dark-toned commentary channel with series formulas
("…Tried To Warn You", "…The Most Evil Business In The World") (`channel-breakdowns.md` §14).
Critics say its videos often overreach their titles; read these as packaging lessons, not as
fact-safety models. Four videos. Labels as in `fern.md`.

## 1. TikTok Is Worse Than You Thought

`https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t7STD2ESmWg` · **8.0M views** · 32:42 · published 2021-10-31 · 356K likes (4.47%)

**Measured** [observed, from captions and YouTube's most-replayed data]: most-replayed peaks 18:18 (1.0), 23:52 (0.82), 28:07 (0.8), 7:31 (0.51).

- **Title and thumbnail:** `[Platform] Is Worse Than You Thought`. Thumbnail: a young woman's face lit red with a Chinese-flag tape over her mouth, TikTok logos glitching behind [observed]. Chromatic-aberration glitch and a gag: strong, but it states a claim visually.
- **Hook (0:00–0:30):** No captions available to us. The description opens "Delete TikTok Before It's Too Late…" and calls it "a psychological weapon" [observed]: maximal claims.
- **Structure:** 12 chapters, 33 minutes: the explosion → "the sinister reality" → ByteDance → expansion → marketing → addiction → data → government response → algorithm and culture → geopolitics → bubble → "preparing for conflict" [observed].
- **Chapters (from the description):** 0:00 The TikTok explosion · 1:09 The sinister reality · 2:25 Rise of ByteDance · 4:31 Global expansion strategy · 7:45 Marketing and acquisition · 8:58 Psychological addiction · 13:18 Data collection threat · 15:23 Government response · 17:58 Algorithm and culture · 20:42 Geopolitical weapon · 26:28 The economic bubble · 28:55 Preparing for conflict
- **Re-hooks and timing:** Not measured. Heat rises in the second half (quarters 0.11, 0.07, 0.26, 0.18) [observed]: the geopolitics section is what people replay.
- **Wow moments:** Top peak 18:18 (Algorithm and culture); 23:52 and 28:07 (geopolitics, conflict) [observed].
- **Ending:** Not measured.
- **What we steal:** **The 'Worse Than You Thought' frame** reopens a belief the viewer already holds about an app they use (outlier factor F2). Don't copy the overreach: our version must deliver evidence for every claim (`fact-check-playbook.md` §5).

## 2. BlackRock: The Most Evil Business In The World

- **Title and thumbnail:** `[Company]: The Most Evil Business In The World`. Thumbnail: a CEO's face with hypnotic spiral glasses and his hands over a globe, the BlackRock wordmark [observed].
- **Hook (0:00–0:30):** "This is a company most people have never heard of and this company quite literally owns the world" then a list of claims about banks, pharma and media [observed]. The size-vs-fame gap (same as MagnatesMedia's Tencent), stated as fact.
- **Structure:** 8 chapters: origins → profit from a crisis → control of the world? → sponsor → world domination (media ownership) → consequences → the future [observed].
- **Chapters (from the description):** 0:00 BlackRock? · 1:25 Origins · 4:15 Profit from a crisis · 6:00 Control of the world? · 6:39 Masterworks · 8:00 World Domination · 10:25 The Consequences · 14:33 The Future
- **Re-hooks and timing:** Turn words every 24 s; the fastest narration in our set (204 wpm) [observed].
- **Wow moments:** Top peak 7:58: "now back to the video" after the sponsor, the skip-landing. 8:17: ownership stakes in media companies listed by percentage [observed].
- **Ending:** A dystopian summary line [observed].
- **What we steal:** **Ownership percentages listed company by company** are a replay magnet; we'd draw them as a network (`visual-metaphors.md` holding-company row). Caution: 'owns' vs 'manages on behalf of clients' is exactly the distinction a fact-check must keep.

## 4. How Instagram Changed Society Forever

`https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1exiUZncNnA` · **3.7M views** · 23:41 · published 2022-07-20 · 112K likes (3.03%)

**Measured** [observed, from captions and YouTube's most-replayed data]: 5,116 caption words, **216 wpm** of runtime (auto captions); turn words ('but', 'however', 'until'…) every **12.1 s** median, first at 0:02; most-replayed peaks 13:44 (1.0), 18:28 (0.44), 12:18 (0.4), 5:26 (0.31).

- **Title and thumbnail:** `How [Platform] Changed Society Forever`. Thumbnail: SOCIETY IS DOOMED in white and red, an influencer posing, Instagram logos glowing [observed].
- **Hook (0:00–0:30):** "Instagram is destroying society. But, Instagram used to be a great site" (a turn at 0:02) then the founder who turned down three tech giants [observed]. Verdict first, then the reversal, in two sentences.
- **Structure:** Rise and fall of a product's values: founders and principles → filters → growth → Facebook acquisition → ads and algorithm → the founders leave → harms to teens (internal research) → data scraping [observed].
- **Chapters:** none published.
- **Re-hooks and timing:** The fastest turn rate in the set (3 per minute, median gap 12 s) at 216 wpm [observed].
- **Wow moments:** Top peak 13:44: "Now, back to the video" after the Honeygain sponsor, the skip-landing. 18:28: Facebook consolidating control. 12:18: the founders' clash over ads [observed].
- **Ending:** Not captured.
- **What we steal:** **Verdict, then reversal, in the first two sentences** ("X is destroying Y. But X used to be great."); a founders-vs-acquirer clash as the centre.
