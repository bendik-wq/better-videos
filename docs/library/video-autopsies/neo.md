# Neo: video autopsies

Neo (`@NeoExplains`) makes calm satellite-and-map documentaries about places, operations and
buildings (`channel-breakdowns.md` §10). Its "drop from orbit onto the place" opener is the oner
in our library brief. Five videos. Labels as in `fern.md`.

## Channel patterns

- **Dated announcements and archive news as cold opens** [observed in 4 of 5].
- **Aerial and satellite imagery is the hero** in four of five thumbnails [observed]; the fifth is the
  case-board grammar shared with Fern.
- **Calm narration with long turn gaps** (43–78 s) [observed]: images carry the pace. This is the
  exception to the 30–40 s re-hook rule, and it works because every shot is a new place or clue.
- **Companion content on Nebula** for the climax [observed].
