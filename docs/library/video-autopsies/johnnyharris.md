# Johnny Harris: video autopsies

Johnny Harris (ex-Vox *Borders*) is first-person, on location and map-heavy
(`channel-breakdowns.md` §5). He's on camera, which we aren't, so the lessons here are about
structure, maps and investigation rather than presence. Five videos. Labels as in `fern.md`.

## Channel patterns

  manual) [observed].
- **First-person investigation** ("I got an email…", "I went to the factory…") [observed]. Our
  faceless equivalent: "We obtained…", "We added up…", used sparingly (`narration-style.md`).

- **Exact odd numbers and parenthetical honesty in titles** ("374,142 Bunkers (and likely more)") [observed].
