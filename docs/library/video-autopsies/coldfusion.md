# ColdFusion: video autopsies

ColdFusion (Dagogo Altraide, Australia) narrates every episode and scores many with his own
music as Burn Water (`channel-breakdowns.md` §12). Five of its biggest business and fraud videos,
from auto or manual captions, chapters and most-replayed data pulled 2026-10-12. Labels as in
`fern.md`.

## Channel patterns

- **News-montage and quote cold opens** [observed in 3 of 5]: real archive voices first.
- **Rise-and-fall fraud stories are the channel's outliers** (Theranos 12.7M, Enron 8.7M, FTX 8.8M,
  Nikola 6.4M against a 0.66M channel median, `channel-breakdowns.md` §0) [data].
- **Long chapter lists (10–14) with plain labels** [observed]; our curiosity-named chapters are an
  upgrade, not a copy.
- **The rewatched beat in money videos is the mechanism** (money creation, mark-to-market) [observed].
- **Speed**: FTX was out five days after the bankruptcy filing [observed].
