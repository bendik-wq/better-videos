# hoog: video autopsies

hoog (Amsterdam; also a Fern narrator) builds isometric 3D models of buildings and cities and
explains the design decisions behind them (`channel-breakdowns.md` §11). It's the closest channel
to our renderer's strengths. Four videos. Labels as in `fern.md`.

## Channel patterns

- **The model is the hero object**: every thumbnail is a 3D render (miniature city, diorama, map) with
  one red element [observed]. Directly our look and our engine.
- **Open on the object, then ask why** [observed in both captioned videos].
- **Steep decline after the first quarter** in the shorter videos [observed]: the model reveal is front-loaded,
  so stagger new model reveals through the video (one per chapter).
