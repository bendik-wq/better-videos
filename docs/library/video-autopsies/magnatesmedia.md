# MagnatesMedia: video autopsies

MagnatesMedia (John, UK) makes long "business mini-movies" with stock and film footage and a
conversational, joking narration (`channel-breakdowns.md` §7). Its biggest videos are 30–80
minutes. Five of them. Labels as in `fern.md`.

## Channel patterns

- **Long (30–80 min), dense (176–214 wpm), joking narration** [observed]. The jokes are pattern
  interrupts; our dry voice uses understatement for the same job (`narration-style.md` P8).
- **The outlier frame is the hidden owner or hidden history of a brand the viewer uses** [observed].
- **Sponsor and subscribe asks are labelled chapters** [observed]; the line after each is a
  skip-landing peak (20:02, 36:31 in Silk Road).
- **Caps words and colons in titles** cluster on this channel and its median is low (0.17M)
  (`channel-breakdowns.md` §0) [data]. Copy the frames, not the caps.
