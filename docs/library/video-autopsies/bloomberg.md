## Channel patterns

- **Access is the packaging**: a famous presenter, a lab, a CEO, a teardown [observed].
- **Short explainers carry the numbers** (Singapore in 7 min, Huawei in 8) [observed].
- **Series suffixes in titles** ("| The Future With Hannah Fry") brand the format [observed]; they
  push the median title length up, so keep ours short.
