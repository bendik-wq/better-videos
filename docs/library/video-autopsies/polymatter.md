## Channel patterns

- **Sponsor in the first sentence, read at the end** (2018–2020 catalogue) [observed].
- **The hook is a computed or per-second number or a misdirection**, and it's in the first 15 s [observed].
- **Steepest heat decline in our set**: the first quarter is 4–10× the last [observed]. PolyMatter
  videos are short (8–13 min) because the hook does most of the work; the channel's recent longer
  videos earn far fewer views (`channel-breakdowns.md` §9).
- **Thumbnail text ≠ title** in 4 of 5 [observed].
