# Vox: video autopsies

Vox's video team set the template for the visual explainer: "no desks", and only make a video if
the story is distinctly visual (`channel-breakdowns.md` §6). Two of its all-time biggest and two
recent money explainers. Labels as in `fern.md`.

## 1. Why no aquarium has a great white shark

`https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QMbHLF_zwjs` · **32.8M views** · 5:54 · published 2016-07-08 · 381K likes (1.16%)

**Measured** [observed, from captions and YouTube's most-replayed data]: 1,011 caption words, **171 wpm** of runtime (manual captions); turn words ('but', 'however', 'until'…) every **25.0 s** median, first at 0:08; most-replayed peaks 4:50 (0.45), 5:07 (0.29), 5:39 (0.22), 2:57 (0.17).

- **Title and thumbnail:** `Why no [place] has a [creature]`, sentence case. Thumbnail: silhouettes of aquarium visitors before a tank with a yellow dashed outline of a shark and a question mark where the shark should be [observed]. **The absence is drawn.**
- **Hook (0:00–0:30):** "There are some shark species that seem to do okay in aquariums… But not the great white" (0:08). Then decades of failed attempts [observed]. A list, then the exception.
- **Structure:** Mystery of an absence in 6 minutes: failed attempts → Monterey Bay's six-month success (a 4-million-gallon ocean pen, a custom transport tank) → why it can't last (breathing, swimming in straight lines, they killed other sharks) → they stopped trying → a resource plug [observed].
- **Chapters (from the description):** 0:00 The difficulty of captivity · 0:49 Monterey Bay's success · 2:13 Breathing and transportation · 2:55 Challenges and limitations · 4:35 Legacy and research
- **Re-hooks and timing:** Turns every 25 s, each 'But…' a new obstacle [observed].
- **Wow moments:** 4:50–5:07: a shark taken to a Japanese aquarium in 2016 died within three days. 2:57: attendance rose 30% while a shark was on display, and the shark was released after killing two others [observed].
- **Ending:** A recommendation of the Biodiversity Heritage Library's shark illustrations [observed].
- **What we steal:** **Draw the missing thing** (a dashed red outline where the object should be): a direct thumbnail grammar for 'why is X empty / missing' stories. The absence hook (`hooks.md` §11).

## Channel patterns

- **Make the absence visible** (the dashed shark) and **let the thumbnail answer what the title
  asks** (IT'S THE BATTERY) [observed].
- **Expert contradiction on camera** ("Is it land scarcity?" "No.") [observed]. Our faceless version:
  a quote card from a named expert or a primary document.
- **Short (6–10 min) and sentence-case titles** for the biggest videos [observed].
