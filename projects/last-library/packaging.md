# The Last Library: packaging

## Angle
**Mystery object + hidden mechanism.** A book with its spine sliced off. The familiar part: AI was trained on "the internet". The unexpected part: the labs ran out, so they bought millions of real books and cut them apart to scan them. And the one library they still can't read sits inside ordinary companies. The video asks one question: *what is the last thing AI hasn't read, and who gets paid for it?*

Evergreen frame: the Library of Alexandria searched every ship in its harbour for books. The same appetite, 2,200 years apart.

## Core / casual / new check
- **Core** (AI, tech and money viewers): yes. The court records, the money and the data wall.
- **Casual** (history and documentary viewers): yes. Alexandria, the sliced books, "they ran out of internet".
- **New** (business owners): yes. "Your company is the last library" is a direct stake for them.
