# Packaging: Episode 7, the Hunt brothers' silver corner

**Why this topic** (idea-engine, 19 Oct 2026):
- **A story.** It has protagonists (the richest family in America), an obsession (inflation), and a heist-like build (buying a third
  of the world's free silver). It also has a villain twist: the exchange board changes the rules. Then come the climax (Silver
  Thursday), the fall (bankruptcy) and an irony ending.
- **Timely.** Silver hit a record of about $121 on 29 Jan 2026, then crashed the next day. The same machine, margin, broke it.
- **High CPM.** Precious metals, investing and markets are among the top-paying ad categories.
- **Under-served.** On YouTube, the best video on the Hunt brothers has about 374k views. The equivalent Soros / Barings / LTCM / Archegos
  videos have 1–4M each.
- **Visual.** Silver is the most photogenic material there is in a bright studio: bars, coins, forks, molten metal, a jet,
  a vault.

## The angle (familiar but unexpected)
- **Familiar:** silver is a boring metal in your grandmother's drawer.
- **Unexpected:**
  - In 1980, two brothers from Texas held so much of it that Americans lined up in the cold to melt that drawer.
  - The exchange changed the rules mid-game, and the richest family in America couldn't pay one phone call.
- **The promise the video must keep:** show how a corner works (and the machine that breaks one), tell what happened to the
  Hunts minute by minute, and show why the same machine broke silver again in January 2026.

## Thumbnails (one hero, one key light, one red annotation, ≤3 words)
- **A, "ALL OF IT" (lead).** A mountain of real-looking cast silver bars in the bright studio, with two tiny clay figures in cowboy hats
  standing on top. Hard key light from the left, long soft shadows. A thin red bracket around the mountain. Text: **ALL OF IT**.
- **B, "$6 → $50".** One hero silver bar, tilted, catching a bright highlight. A red arrow climbs over it and then snaps
  down. Text: **$6 → $50**.
- **C, the fork.** Grandma's silver fork melting into a glowing crucible, with a bar mould beside it. Text: **WHY?**

## Hero object
**A cast 1,000-oz silver bar** (stamped FINE SILVER 999.0). It carries the thumbnail, the cold open (a fork melts into a
bar) and the climax (the bar tower collapses on Silver Thursday). The secondary hero is the **rotary phone**: the margin call.
