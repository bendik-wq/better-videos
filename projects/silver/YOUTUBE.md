## Description

> In January 1980, Americans lined up in the cold to melt down their grandmothers' silver. A year earlier an ounce cost about $6. Now it cost $50, and the reason, more than anything, was two brothers from Dallas. Nelson Bunker Hunt and Herbert Hunt, with their partners, controlled about 195 million ounces, worth almost $10 billion on paper. Ten weeks later they couldn't pay their broker. This is the story of how a corner works, the hidden lever that breaks one (margin), and why the same lever was pulled when silver crashed again in January 2026.
