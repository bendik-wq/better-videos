## The structure (all seven follow it)

1. **Hook line, 0–4 s.** One sentence that names a broken status quo or a big claim:
   - "The way everyone is building with AI is fundamentally flawed." (Pine)
   - "Right now, AI is killing our kids' ability to think." (Koji)
   - "I co-created ChatGPT and RLHF." (TypeSafe: the credibility *is* the hook)
2. **Who's talking, 3–8 s.** A name plus title lower third, small and quiet, shown once.
3. **The old way / the problem, ~15–30 s.** Shown with a simple diagram or a struck-through idea, not just talked about.
4. **The insight, one line.** "The model isn't the bottleneck anymore." "Instead of words…"
5. **Introducing X.** A clean title card or a headline over the speaker. Brand colour on one word.
6. **Proof, the longest section, 30–60 s.** The product works on camera, live:
   - Fish: a real pizza order phone call, with a latency readout on screen.
   - Koji: a screen recording with the cursor and a kid's face cam.
   - Tavus: the video itself is the demo.
   - Wispr: a real-life montage.
7. **Numbers, 10–20 s.** One stat per card ("5× faster", "$1.00 → $0.05", "48%"). Use a benchmark bar chart where "us" is the only coloured bar.
8. **Offer + CTA built for X.**
   - "Like, comment, repost for a free month." (Fish)
   - "Comment what you want your kid to learn." (Koji)
   - "Join the waitlist." (Pine)
9. **Button.** A joke or human beat right before the logo:
   - Fish: a "One hour later…" card.
   - Tavus: "Is it too much? No, this is it."
   - TypeSafe: a fake "DISCLAIMER" card.
   - Wispr: a celebration shot.
10. **Logo end card**, 2–8 s, on plain white or black.

## The look

**Camera**
- Cinematic interview framing: shallow depth of field and warm practical light.
- The founder wears a plain tee and sits in a designed room (wood, bookshelves, a leather chair).
- Two angles, front and 3/4 profile, cut on every sentence or two. The angle change *is* the rhythm.

**Kinetic type in the scene**
- Words appear word by word, in sync with the voice, placed in the empty side of the frame beside the speaker.
- Set in a big light sans, tight tracking, white.
- Sometimes it sits *behind* the head (TypeSafe, Pine), which needs a person matte.

**Colour**
- One brand accent only (TypeSafe pink, Pine mint, Koji green).
- It's used on one word, one bar or one outline box. Everything else is white, black or grey.

**Graphic elements**
- Thin outline boxes around one word or label.
- Corner-bracket "viewfinder" frames.
- Small mono labels.
- Hand-drawn connector lines from the speaker to a callout.

**Full-screen inserts**
- Every 15–25 s, cut to a full-frame graphic for one concept: a diagram, a UI mock or a chart.
- Backgrounds are light grey/white (Pine, Koji) or the brand colour (TypeSafe).
- Each insert is on screen 3–8 s.

**Captions**
- Always on: small, centred and low, in a soft dark pill or plain white.
- They're secondary, never the main graphic.

**Sound**
- Light music bed under the voice.
- Swells on "Introducing".
- Small UI clicks/whooshes on graphic hits.
- A hard silence before the button joke.

## What this means for Josh

Josh's footage is one locked seated camera, unscripted, about 9 minutes. To get the launch feel:

- **Script-cut, don't trim.** Pull the 25–35 strongest sentences into a 2-minute arc using the structure above. Hook, problem, insight, "here's how", proof (deal numbers), offer and CTA. Tighter than our clips: no breath pauses.
- **Fake the second camera.** Alternate a wide crop and a tight crop from the 4K source on every sentence. Slight push-ins on key lines.
- **Kinetic type beside him**, word-synced. Use our min style's crisp type with a single accent colour, plus outline boxes and corner brackets instead of scribbles.
- **Full-screen light inserts** for the deal-structure ideas: vendor finance flow, a profit ≠ cash chart, a "$500K offer vs real value" bar.
- **Numbers cards:**
  - "9 / 10 owners won't do a deal"
  - "100% vendor finance"
  - "Six figures out on day one"
- **X-native CTA + button:** "Comment DEAL and I'll send you the breakdown", then a human beat from the footage.
