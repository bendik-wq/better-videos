# Packaging: Episode 9, the Supergun (Gerald Bull)

## Angle
- **Familiar:** an unsolved assassination of a man who built a weapon for a dictator.
- **Unexpected:** the weapon started as a space cannon. HARP's 16-inch gun put a Martlet 2 to about 180 km on 18 Nov 1966. This was at **Yuma, Arizona, not Barbados**: Barbados was the main HARP site, but the altitude record shot was fired at Yuma (BRL memo by Murphy & Bull, Feb 1967). And the gun's barrel was found as "petroleum pipes".
- **Frames:** mystery object (the barrel disguised as pipes), superlative (a 1 m bore), villain or contrarian (the genius the West dropped), and stakes ladder (space, then felony, then Saddam, then the bullet, then Teesport, then Whitehall).
- **Cold-open mechanism:** a dated present-tense scene (Brussels, 22 Mar 1990), then a flashback to 1966.

## C/C/N
- **Core:** yes (geopolitics plus engineering).
- **Casual:** yes (Saddam plus an assassination plus a giant gun).
- **New:** yes. "Space cannon" and "oil pipes" need no prior knowledge. Bull's name is unknown, so **keep his name out of titles**.

## Fact locks for packaging
- **Shot count:** sources disagree (2, 3 or 5). Don't put a number in the title, thumbnail or hook.
- **$20,000 in cash:** NYT via Reuters, 1990. Say "about $20,000".
- **Teesport:** about 11 Apr 1990, eight tube sections by Sheffield Forgemasters, labelled petroleum pipes, bound for Iraq. Some sources say "about two weeks" after the killing, so say "weeks later", or "three weeks" only if SOURCES fixes the date.
- **Killers:** unknown. Mossad, Iraq and others are **suspected** only.
- **"Britain knew":** use the 1992 Trade & Industry Committee report on Project Babylon plus the Scott Report (1996). The wording must be "had been warned" or "knew more than they said", not "hid it".
- **Babylon:** about 1 m bore, about 150 m long, never completed. Baby Babylon (350 mm) was built.

## Revision 3 (two-critic pass, 7.5 and 6): what changed and why
- **Demand note corrected.** The v2 claim that Teesport is uncovered was a keyword artifact. Qxir's "The Supergun Affair" (611K, 2020) covers Forgemasters, Walter Somers, Hal Miller MP, Ridley, MI6 and the failed prosecution. The topic has at least 8 videos at 150K+ views: Real Engineering 2.58M, Qxir 611K, Megaprojects 385K / 323K / 172K, Banijay 315K, Infographics Show 164K. Its realistic ceiling so far is about 2.6M. What nobody owns is the **image**: every competitor packages a gun or a face, and none packages the pipes. The edge is packaging (mystery object) plus primary records on screen (pattern 12), not the topic itself.
- **Lead pair flipped to the mystery object.** T1 is now "Why Were These Oil Pipes Going to Saddam?" The old T1 ("...Then Someone Shot Him") matched AI-channel titles (DeadlyBlueprints, The Wrong Future), gave the murder away, and promised a whodunit the ending can't solve. It is retired from the test. The new T1 promises only what the video fully pays off: why the pipes were going to Iraq, and who in Britain knew.
- **Thumbnails rebuilt.** A (side-on barrel) read as a pipeline at 168x94, its scale read as ordinary artillery, and FOR SADDAM was the expected fact. B relied on wall thickness, which only an engineer notices. C's "1 METRE" undersold the object, and a seated figure read as a culvert. New A merges B's reveal with C's scale trick: a person crouching inside the "pipe". New B uses forced perspective down the muzzle. New C is a pipe-vs-barrel matchup.
- **Tone.** House rules keep the bright blue sky and white studio, so the grade comes from the light, not the sky: a hard, low late-afternoon sun, long black shadows, weathered matte steel, splintered wood and matte (not glossy) mannequins. That reads as a thriller still, not a kids' diorama.
- **Hook rewritten.** It opens on the crate (the thumbnail image) and turns in sentence 2 ("They aren't pipes"). The $20K gets its own beat. It ends on a concrete Britain loop (an MP's warning) instead of "never proven". Space, the 1980 felony, Israel, Baby Babylon and the satellite claim are **held back** as midpoint climaxes.
- **Britain climax anchored to a primary record.** Hal Miller MP's April 1990 Commons statement that he had warned the government in 1988 appears on screen with a red loop on the key line, backed by the 1992 T&I Committee report and the 1996 Scott Report. **VERIFY the exact Hansard wording and date before scripting.**
- **Overclaim fixed.** Babylon "was designed to" put payloads into orbit (full orbit needed rocket-assisted projectiles). It "could have" is never said.
- **Prediction re-baselined** to this topic's real range.

## Fact locks added in v3 (VERIFY before script lock)
- **Hal Miller MP:** said in the Commons (Apr 1990) that in 1988 he had told government departments about the Walter Somers orders. Check this in Hansard.
- **Earlier shipments:** other tube sections from British firms had already reached Iraq before the Teesport seizure, and the hook line "not the first to leave" depends on it. Confirm with T&I 1992 / Scott. If it can't be confirmed, swap in the fallback line given in the hook notes.
- **Timing:** "less than three weeks" after 22 Mar is safe whether the seizure was ~5 Apr or 11 Apr. Never say "twenty days".
- **"Petroleum":** the declaration wording on the crates/paperwork, which the thumbnail stencils. Check that it was "petroleum" (vs "petrochemical"). If it was "petrochemical", the stencil changes to PETROCHEMICAL, which is still 1 word.

## Chosen pair
**"Why Were These Oil Pipes Going to Saddam?"** with **A (person inside the pipe, PETROLEUM)**.

**Hook (first ~30 s, word for word):**
> April 1990. Customs officers in the north of England open a crate of oil pipes bound for Iraq. They aren't pipes. They're sections of a gun a hundred and fifty metres long, designed for Saddam Hussein. Less than three weeks earlier, the man who designed it was shot dead at his own front door in Brussels. He had about twenty thousand dollars on him. [beat] His killer left it. [beat] And two years before this crate was opened, a British MP had warned the government exactly what these pipes were. This crate was not the first to leave.

Fallback last line, if earlier shipments can't be confirmed: "So why was anyone surprised?"

**Visuals for 0:00–0:30:** 0–3 s, the crate lid comes off on the thumbnail tube (A, exact). At "They aren't pipes", a crouching figure inside. At "a hundred and fifty metres", the camera pulls back and the sections assemble into the barrel on the hillside (first climax, about 0:10). At "front door in Brussels", a doorway at night with a police cordon. At "twenty thousand dollars", a cash envelope with a red loop. At "a British MP", the Hansard page with the line about to be looped.

**Open loops:** L1 (main): who in Britain knew, and what they did. This is paid off at the end with the Hansard line on screen, then T&I 1992, then Scott 1996. L2: why a killer leaves $20,000, which points to a professional hit. Suspects are weighed in Act 5 (Mossad, Iraq, others), suspected only, and the video admits it is unproven (the LEMMiNO pattern). L3: who designs a 150 m gun? This is answered at about 0:45 by the space reveal.

**Held back as midpoint climaxes (not in the hook):** HARP's shell **into space** (~180 km, Yuma, 18 Nov 1966), placed at about 0:45 as the first post-hook re-hook; Canada and the US pulling funding; the 1980 guilty plea (shells to South Africa); the 1988 Babylon contract; Baby Babylon actually built; the capability fear ("designed to" put payloads into orbit; analysts feared a shell reaching Israel); the warnings and break-ins.

**Promise list:** the crate and tube from thumbnail A are on screen in 0–3 s; "petroleum" or "oil pipes" bound for Iraq, April 1990; the sections are a ~150 m gun designed for Saddam (never completed); the designer was shot at his front door in Brussels, 22 Mar 1990 (no shot count); about $20,000 left on him; a British MP warned the government in 1988 (Hansard on screen); earlier sections had already shipped (VERIFY); the killers are suspected, never stated; **end** on the Hansard line, then cut back to the open crate and the empty tube on the quay, which is thumbnail A without the figure.

**Prediction (re-baselined):** CTR 4–7%; 7 days 50K–150K; 28 days 150K–450K. Upside past 600K only if the pipes thumbnail beats the "supergun" visuals that browse serves next to Real Engineering's 2.58M.
