# look-lab assets

- `hormuz.json`: Natural Earth 1:50m land, lakes, coastline and land boundaries (public domain,
  https://www.naturalearthdata.com), taken from `projects/palantir/assets/geo/vectors.json` (see its
  SOURCES.md) and clipped to lon 44–65, lat 18–33. Credit: "Made with Natural Earth."
- Footage in the `ship` set: `ships-lcs-formation` (US Navy / Keith DeVinney, public domain; already in
  `assets/CREDITS.md`). Aerial: USDA NAIP + USGS 3DEP `pentagon` set (public domain; in `assets/CREDITS.md`).
- The chart series is illustrative test data only (labelled on screen), not for publication.
