# Format Dissection: Max Sturtevant — “I Studied Three $100M Brands. Their Strategies Contradict Each Other”

## 1) Packaging: Why Title + Thumbnail Work

**Title:**  
“I Studied Three $100M Brands. Their Strategies Contradict Each Other”

The title works because:

- **Specific social proof:** “Three $100M Brands” is concrete. It promises a high-level insight.
- **“I Studied”** implies labor, research, and authority—not just opinion.
- **“Their Strategies Contradict Each Other”** introduces cognitive dissonance. Viewers expect one playbook; the title says the opposite. That makes them ask: “If the strategies contradict, which one is right for me?”
- **Curiosity gap:** The viewer is left wondering how three successful brands can use opposing strategies and still win. The implied risk is “Copy one without understanding why and you could go wrong.”

**Thumbnail:**  
Three products on a shelf, each with a yellow revenue tag, under a bold “HOW THEY GROW” headline.

Why it works:

- **Visual pattern:** Three products mirrors the “three brands” promise. The brain instantly groups them as three case studies.
- **Yellow revenue tags** look like price tags or growth tags—money/revenue is visually anchored onto each product.
- **“HOW THEY GROW”** is a promise, not a tease. It says: this video will reverse-engineer grown-up brands.
- **Shelf context** signals consumer brands—tangible products—rather than abstract B2B.
- **Overall promise:** You will see how each brand grows, but the “contradiction” in the title means you’ll also learn *which* lever to pull and *why*.

## 2) Beat Sheet With Timestamps

### 0:00–0:38 — Hook / Prologue

The first 30 seconds are a tight contradiction tease.

Exact quote from the hook:

> “I spent the last few weeks studying these multi-nine-figure brands, and all of them have reached their growth in completely different ways. Ridge has one completely unique method to reach new customers and get retention. Groon has a completely different method, and they just sell one product. And Blume has a completely different strategy from those brands, and they’re scaling at record pace. So, copying one of these brands without fully understanding why they’re doing what they’re doing could completely throw your brand in the wrong direction.”

Then immediately:

- **Credibility line:**  
  “Now, I can add a lot of context to this cuz I work with several nine-figure brands, and I run retention for over 100 different brands at my agency, Well Traveled Coffee.”

- **Promise line:**  
  “In this video, I’m going to be breaking down exactly what these brands are doing so you can learn why so you can apply it to your business where it makes sense.”

|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:21 | Contradictory setup: Ridge vs. Gruns vs. Bloom | Curiosity gap |
| 0:21–0:26 | Warning: “Copying one of these brands without fully understanding why… could completely throw your brand” | Stakes |
| 0:26–0:31 | Credibility: agency, 100+ brands, nine-figure brands | Authority |

### 14:32–15:17 — Contradiction Payoff / Synthesis

> “So I made this video to prove there’s not one playbook that you follow.”

The video summarizes acquisition and retention separately:

- Ridge: acquisition = new products; retention = new products/styles
- Gruns: acquisition = same product, different funnels; retention = consumable product + systems
- Bloom: acquisition = 50K+ TikTok affiliates; retention = retail everywhere

This is the “contradiction” payoff: no single strategy wins, but each brand solved acquisition and retention differently.

### 15:17–16:13 — Viewer Application + Final CTA

This is where the video connects the cases back to the viewer’s business.

At 15:17:

Then:

> “What you can do is figure out what sort of general approach is right for your brand and take little pieces from each funnel that you like and make sense.”

Then he shifts to “know your numbers”:

- Cohort performance
- Customer worth by acquisition month
- Time between orders
- Product type filtering

**Final CTA placement:**

- 15:49: “If you need a retention partner, you should definitely click the link below to work with us.”
- 15:59: Next-video CTA: “Watch this video I have next which breaks down a $100 million e-commerce brand offers…”

## 5) Reusable Template With Slot Names

Use this structure for any “three contradictory examples” video.

### `[VIDEO_TITLE]`

“I Studied Three `[HIGH-VALUE UNIT]` `[GROUP]`. Their `[STRATEGY/METHOD]` Contradict Each Other.”

Example:  
“I Studied Three `7-Figure` `YouTube Channels`. Their `Growth Strategies` Contradict Each Other.”

---

### `[HOOK]` — 0:00–0:30

- Name three examples.
- Give each one a distinct, simple strategy.
- Create contradiction.
- Warn against copying blindly.

Quote pattern:

> “`[Case A]` has one completely unique method. `[Case B]` has a completely different method. And `[Case C]` is doing something completely different. Copying one of these without understanding why could throw your `[audience]` in the wrong direction.”

---

### `[CREDIBILITY]` — 0:20–0:30

- State relevant experience.
- Use a number.

Example:

> “I work with `[relevant clients]`, and I `[specific work]` for over `[number]` different `[clients/brands/clients]`.”

---

### `[PROMISE]` — 0:28–0:38

- Tell the viewer exactly what they will learn.
- Include “why” and “where it applies.”

Quote pattern:

> “In this video, I’m breaking down exactly what these `[examples]` are doing so you can learn why and so you can apply it to your `[business/channel/project]` where it makes sense.”

---

### `[CASE_1]` — 4–6 minutes

**Slot order:**

1. **Product/context**  
   Open with the core subject.
2. **Personal entry**  
   “I actually have this `[product/tool/thing]`.”
3. **Old model**  
   What they used to do.
4. **Limitations**  
   Enumerate 2–3 specific problems.
   - `[Seasonality]`
   - `[Low repeat usage]`
   - `[Narrow audience]`
5. **Turning point / open loop**  
   “How did they solve this?”
6. **Founder/CEO/inside evidence**  
   Use a quote or clip.
7. **Mechanism/data**  
   Show the numbers behind why it worked.
8. **Wider implication**  
   Explain the strategic unlock.
9. **Application filter**  
   When should the viewer copy this?
10. **Early CTA**  
   Soft embedded CTA related to the content.

---

### `[TRANSITION_TO_CASE_2]` — 0:10–0:20

- Directly contrast the first case.

Quote pattern:

> “`[Case A]` grew by `[method]`. But `[Case B]` is worth `[number]` and never did that. They follow a completely different playbook.”

---

### `[CASE_2]` — 4–5 minutes

**Slot order:**

1. **Contradiction intro**  
   Opposite of Case 1.
2. **Product/market logic**  
   Why the core product structurally retains or attracts.
3. **Category context**  
   Compare to old alternatives.
4. **Problem**  
   The scaling ceiling.
5. **Disclaimer if needed**  
   “I’m not inside the company… take this with a grain of salt.”
6. **Diagnosis**  
   One sharp insight.
7. **Specific examples**  
   Funnels, audiences, offers, pain points.
8. **Summary contrast**  
   “`[Case A]` did X; `[Case B]` did Y.”

---

### `[MID_ROLL_CTA]` — 30 seconds

Place after two full cases.

Use structure:

- “But before that…”
- Community/course/template promise
- Number of members
- Specific assets
- Direct access
- Link in description

---

### `[CASE_3]` — 3–4 minutes

**Slot order:**

1. **Intro/history**  
   Show the arc from beginning to breakout.
2. **Big tease**  
   “Their biggest unlock is something they don’t even sell online.”
3. **Product/channel swing**  
   New product, new channel, or new distribution.
4. **Category wedge**  
   Who they targeted and how they stood out.
5. **Personal proof**  
   “I see this everywhere. I use it every day.”
6. **Why they can ignore optimization**  
   Example: “Their website isn’t even optimized, but they don’t care because…”
7. **Case summary**  
   One sentence tying acquisition and retention.

---

### `[SYNTHESIS / CONTRADICTION PAYOFF]` — 45 seconds

- Create a comparison table or verbal summary.
- Split into acquisition vs. retention.
- Land the core idea.

Quote pattern:

> “So I made this video to prove there’s not one playbook that you follow.”

---

### `[VIEWER_APPLICATION]` — 30–45 seconds

- Tell the viewer not to blindly copy any one example.
- Tell them to steal pieces.
- Shift to measurement.

Quote pattern:

> “Figure out what sort of general approach is right for your brand and take little pieces from each funnel that you like and make sense.”

Then say:

> “But what’s important is you need to know your numbers.”

List:

- Cohort performance
- Customer value by month
- Time between orders
- Product-type filters

---

### `[FINAL_CTA]` — 15–30 seconds

- Service CTA: “If you need a partner/help, click below.”
- Next-video CTA: “Watch this video next…”
- Keep the loop open.
