## The angle (familiar but unexpected)

- **Familiar:** "AI is a bubble." Everyone has heard it.
- **Unexpected:** the money goes in a circle. The supplier invests in the customer, the customer buys the supplier's chips, and the supplier now guarantees the customer's rent. A company did exactly this in 2000 and went from $84 to 58 cents.
- **The promise:** how the machine works, who holds the risk (including your index fund), and exactly how it could pop, with the strongest counter-case.
