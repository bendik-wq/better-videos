**Angle (familiar but unexpected):** everyone knows the Panama Canal connects two oceans. It actually connects both oceans to a lake 85 ft in the sky, a lake filled only by rain and poured into the sea one ship at a time. The rain is failing just as a historic El Niño arrives.

**Core / casual / new:**
