# VISUALS: America Lost the Same H-Bomb Twice (episode 9)

One visual idea per numbered line of `SCRIPT.md` (revision 8, lines 1–140: 20 and 35 split into a/b, 44 and 94 retired; all other numbers unchanged). This is the brief for `shot-design`, which picks camera, lens, transitions and timing. Set names refer to `SETS-3d.md` (`sky`, `bomb`, `village`, `sea`, `grid`, `alvin`, `trawler`) and `SETS-graphics.md` (`title`, `chapter`, `quote`, `bignumber`, `counter`, `timeline`, `depth`, `map`, `doc`, `photo`).

**Toolkit tags:** **3D** = diorama on the painted sky-blue backdrop (white models, glossy mannequins, no likenesses) · **SEA** = vertical sea cutaway · **MAP** · **DOC** = recreated document (always labelled RECREATED) · **CTR** = split-flap counter · **CHART** · **QUOTE** = quote card · **MANN** = mannequin vignette.

**★ HERO** marks the moments that deserve a bespoke 3D set: new build, extra modelling, custom light. Everything else reuses an existing set or mode.

**Fact locks for every frame (RESEARCH.md):**
- Alvin is **white** (1966).
- Bomb 4's chute is **off-white / dirty yellow**, 64 ft.
- On **24 March** Alvin is **not** on scene. The only light at the bottom is POODL's **strobe**. It is night.
- The line that parted is the **anchor-to-grapnel line** below the anchor, not a hawser at a hull. The bomb "fell, then slid", 120 yd SSW, **+250 ft**.
- Night shots (24 Mar) and the moon and sunset skies (82, 83, 112) carry a **RECONSTRUCTION** tag.
- The bomb is "more than a ton". Never print the variant ("RI"/"FI").
- Moran-only dialogue carries "as told to Barbara Moran". NYT lines carry "NYT reported (2016)". ⏱ items carry an AS OF pill.
- No stock B-52 footage, no real faces.
