A/B title: "America Lost an H-Bomb. Then Lost It Again". Narration: `SCRIPT.md`, 144 lines, **2,818 words, about 18:50 at 150 wpm** (18:58 with 2 s chapter cards; later timestamps shift up to 4 s). Timestamps below use that basis. Line numbers (L#) are SCRIPT.md line numbers.

## 0. How this structure was chosen

The judges' scores: dual 88.7, thriller 84.7, puzzle 76.7. **The spine is the dual braid.** It is the only one that seeds the land story before the bomb comes up, so the last act pays off loops the viewer is already carrying.

**Grafted in from the other two outlines:**
- **From thriller:**
  - the split-flap DAY counter on a DAY 0 basis;
  - the parachute as a physical thread through every chapter;
  - Kuralt's "mislay" line followed by "Two days after Kuralt's broadcast, they had mislaid it";
  - Guest's moonless-night metaphor paid off on 24 March;
  - the double reversal in Ch.5 (fouled, then the robot is the third line).
- **From puzzle:**
  - the red dashed outline of bomb #4: it fills at FOUND, empties at the snap and fills for good on deck;
  - the "recovered today" press release drafted early, which fades as the line goes slack;
  - "So did the Navy ignore him? No.";
  - the cliff "seventy-degree slope, at the lip of a canyon. Finding it was the easy part.";
  - "The calculator hadn't found it."

**Weaknesses the judges named, and how the script fixes them:**

| Weakness | Fix |
|---|---|
| Silent pre-roll before the hook (thriller) | Cut. The hook starts on frame 1. The flash-forward lives only under L8. |
| Ch.1 replays the collision after the title card | Ch.1 opens on the dated scene and reaches the collision at 1:18. The escapes are compressed into 4 lines, and the cause dispute is cut. |
| Ch.2 listing pace | One line per beat. The blackout is 2 lines. The chart, Lotsman and storm beats are cut to one line or dropped. |
| Forced cut-away to land at 5:30 (dual) | The land thread enters through the swim, at L59 "The plutonium was in the fields behind the beach". The camera pulls back from the swimmers to the fields, so it is a motivated move, not a thread switch. It runs 50 s and returns on "Out at sea, the hunt was dying". |
| The 24 Mar land tag could cool the snap | Shrunk to 2 lines (L97–98), 20 s after the snap. It ends on the strongest braid line. |
| Ch.5 coda sag after the lift | Four beats were cut ("extremely dull", the wood screw, Pantex, Thule moved). The coda after L118 is 30 s: "Nobody cheered" → Simó → Scorpion → cliff. |
| Policy pile-up in Ch.6 | Each policy beat is a reversal in one line: L132 → 133 → 134 → 135 → 137. Two counterweight lines keep it fair (L136 dose, the regulator and "could stir up radioactive material"; L142 the village mayor). |
