# Sources: Palomares (America Lost the Same H-Bomb Twice)

Clean source list for `RESEARCH.md`. The IDs match the citations there. Six researcher passes (crash, land, search, politics, people, visual) compiled this list on **2026-10-20/21**. Most URLs were opened in that window. Any exceptions are flagged.

**Tier** (playbook)
- **T1**: primary: original government record, report, log, filing, court order, data set
- **T2**: official: government agency publication or statistics
- **T3**: on-record words: speeches, transcripts, participant memoirs, oral histories
- **T4**: institutional: peer-reviewed, labs, museums, think tanks, archives
- **T5**: quality press and books
- **T6**: aggregators: Wikipedia, blogs, fan sites, catalogue scrapes

The people researcher used a different scale (T2 = 1966 press, T4 = sourced book). It has been mapped onto the playbook here: Moran is **T5** (built on T1 FOIA records and T3 interviews), and 1966 press is **T5**.

**Read status**
- **direct**: opened and read (in full unless marked *partial*).
- **extract**: read through a mirror, a repost, or a quotation inside another source we opened.
- **via Moran** / **via Sxx**: the original was not opened. Its content is known only because Moran (S40) or another opened source cites it, usually by page. Treat it as ⚑ until the original is pulled.
- **blocked**: tried, and the request was refused (403, 503, Cloudflare, rate limits).
- **not online**: print or FOIA only.
- **snippet**: seen only in a search-engine summary. ⚑ Open it before any line rests on it.

**Blocked in this environment:** apps.dtic.mil, all .mil mirrors, usni.org, archive.org/Wayback, whoi.edu, Google Books, elpais.com (one researcher got through once), safety.af.mil, nuclearmuseum.pastperfectonline.com, aviation-safety.net, navsource (via curl).
