## Description
At 6:18 p.m. on 15 April 2019, a screen beside Notre-Dame flashed one word: FEU. The alarm worked. Its message named two attics.

The search began in the wrong one. By the time firefighters reached the roof, the 800-year-old oak "forest" was lost, and a new clock had started: fire had reached the wooden frame holding the north tower's eight bells. If that tower went, officials feared, the cathedral could follow. A French minister said afterwards it came down to fifteen to thirty minutes.

This animated reconstruction follows both clocks, built from the Paris prosecutor's and government timelines, the fire brigade's own accounts and The New York Times' reconstruction. Nobody is blamed: the cause is still undetermined and no one has been charged.
