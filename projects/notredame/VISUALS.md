- The danger is the **burning oak frame**: flame on the beams, never on or from the bells. The domino is captioned "officials' fear" every time (L6, L64). **The bell never slips off its pin** in any frame, the cold open included: the fear was never a near-event.

- Cause: no worker, cigarette, firm logo, scaffold crew or electrical cabinet drawn as a cause. Hypotheses only as words on the candor card.
- **Night plates:** L101 only, plus the dusk tint of L43–95 (STYLE-BIBLE §1.4 night ration). A night plate is **paper night: kraft under a navy halftone**, never a glowing ember on deep navy (README §6.1, Kurzgesagt ban).

- **Fire is never red (rev 4, STYLE-BIBLE fire spec).** Flame is brass-light paper, always. When a line's RED is about fire, it is a **red marker loop around** the brass flame card, never a red flame (L22, L30, L34, L62, L83, L86, L102).
- **Red lock for H5 (new in rev 3).** The bell-frame toy's "red bell" has two states: **dormant** (brass, with an ink dashed ring) and **red**. It is red **only** on lines whose RED: names the bell. On every other line where the toy is in shot, it sits dormant, so a frame never holds two reds.
