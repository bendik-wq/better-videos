## Fact locks for every frame (RESEARCH.md)
- **No sectioned real building, ever** (README §6.4). The cathedral is a folded-card model; to look inside we lift a roof lid and look down. The bell frame is a separate brass-and-card toy on the table. **The tower climb is a separate flat "stair strip" toy beside the closed model** (S-tower strip → bridge tab → N-tower strip), never an opened tower with a route inside.
- No photos, NYT graphics, survey drawings, Rebâtir photos or Pathé frames. Everything redrawn.
- **The security post is in a small separate building (the presbytery)**, never inside the nave.
- **The person at the screen and the guard are faceless card figures, never named, never shown failing.** Red never lands on either. The security firm is never named or given a logo. Staffing appears only as a roster card (L19), never as one person's fatigue.
- **Panel strings, exactly and in this order:** `FEU` · `ATTIC NAVE SACRISTY` · `ZDA-110-3-15-1` · `ASPIRATING FRAMEWORK` (S41 ✓). Card "NYT, 16 Jul 2019". The code is on the panel only; the VO doesn't say it.

Never 33 min, 19:05, 19:44, 18:58.

The Nuñez bracket `¼ h – ½ h · MARGIN (estimate)` lands on the **right wheel only, with chip "which minutes: not stated"**. - **NYT headline card** (✓): "Notre-Dame Came Far Closer to Collapsing Than People Knew" · NYT, 16 Jul 2019, redrawn as plain type (no NYT layout, font or logo).
- The spire is never shown punching through the crossing vault when it falls. The crossing hole appears only in L101, **untimed**, with both tags (⚠ D29).
- The danger is the **burning oak frame**: flame on the beams, never on or from the bells. The domino is captioned "officials' fear" every time (L6, L64). **The bell never slips off its pin** in any frame, the cold open included: the fear was never a near-event.
- Bell weights as a range only (≈ 0.78 t – 4.1 t, Rebâtir). Emmanuel is not drawn (cut from the VO).
- Tower team: no names, no faces, no team declining. The trapped box (L45–46) and the tower team (L76) are **different groups**, drawn in different colours (ink tallies vs teal tallies) so nobody reads them as the same men.
- Cause: no worker, cigarette, firm logo, scaffold crew or electrical cabinet drawn as a cause. Hypotheses only as words on the candor card.
- **Night plates:** L101 only, plus the dusk tint of L43–95 (STYLE-BIBLE §1.4 night ration). A night plate is **paper night: kraft under a navy halftone**, never a glowing ember on deep navy (README §6.1, Kurzgesagt ban).
- Flame is confined to bands through the matchstick bays with paper and ink beyond (§1.3 brass ≤ 8 %).
- **Red lock for H5 (new in rev 3).** The bell-frame toy's "red bell" has two states: **dormant** (brass, with an ink dashed ring) and **red**. It is red **only** on lines whose RED: names the bell. On every other line where the toy is in shot, it sits dormant, so a frame never holds two reds.
