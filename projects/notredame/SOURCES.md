# Sources: Notre-Dame (Notre-Dame Was 30 Minutes From Collapse)

This is the source list for `RESEARCH.md`, and the IDs match the citations there. It was compiled on **2026-11-05** from four researcher passes (timeline, structure, firefight, after) and two adversarial verification passes. Most URLs were opened in that window; exceptions are flagged.

**Tier (channel rule for EP10)**
- **T1**: primary record or official body: the Paris prosecutor, BSPP, Élysée/government, Senate/Assemblée, Ministry of Culture/DRAC, Établissement public Rebâtir Notre-Dame de Paris, Ville de Paris, CMN, Santé publique France, ARS/Préfecture, and the Diocese of Paris (church record)
- **T3**: on-record words: briefings, interviews, signed expert articles
- **T5**: quality press
- **T6**: Wikipedia and aggregators. These are **pointers only**.

**Read status**
- **direct**: opened and read.
- **syndication**: the licensed reprint of a paywalled original, opened and read in full.
- **extract**: read through a quotation or summary inside another source we opened.
- **snippet**: seen only in a search-engine result or excerpt. ⚑ Open it before any line rests on it.
- **blocked**: tried and refused (paywall, 402/403/404, Anubis, 503).
- **not opened**.
- "(V)" means a verifier re-opened it on 2026-11-05. "(V✗)" means the verifier could not re-open it.

**Blocked in this environment:** nytimes.com, lemonde.fr, theguardian.com, x.com (402), europe1.fr (403), artdaily.com (now 404), theborneopost.com (403), hal.science (Anubis wall, for the verifier only), images.cnrs.fr (503).
