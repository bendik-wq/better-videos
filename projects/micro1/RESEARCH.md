## Do not claim
- **Not "guaranteed" pay, and not "$200K–$2M".** Use the published tiers ("$100K+, $500K+, $1M+, depending on…"). Every money figure carries "reported tiers · not guaranteed".
- **No "paid in X days" or "fast money".** No timeline is published. We *can* say that the budget is set for the next 12 months, that the form takes about 2 minutes, and that the money is there now.
- **Not "never customer data".** micro1 lists "CRM and workflow metadata", so say: "the focus is how your company works, not who your customers are. micro1 says sensitive and customer information is scrubbed, and none of it is exposed or shared."
- **Not "micro1 sells your data to OpenAI" or any other named lab.** Say "to train and evaluate AI systems".
- **No patient records or PHI for healthcare.** Healthcare *administration* processes only.
- **Not "we're partners with micro1".** We are independent. The end card carries the disclaimer.
- **Legal:** always say "check your client contracts and talk to your counsel before licensing anything" (FAQ 8). It reads as trustworthy, not as a negative.

## Positives to lean on
- **The money is committed and the window is clear:** $1B, 12 months, backed by Citi and Hercules Capital.
- **It's a new revenue line from something you already have:** documents, playbooks and process knowledge built over years.
- **Your normal operations aren't disrupted.** micro1 says it handles coordination, anonymization and program management, so teams can take part "with minimal disruption" (S1).
- **You keep ownership.** Originals are deleted after processing.
- **Mid-size teams (30–200) are the sweet spot.** This isn't only for enterprises.
- **Applying takes about 2 minutes.**
