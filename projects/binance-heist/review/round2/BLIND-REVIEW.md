## 7. Verdict: REVISE — top 5 changes

1. **Put a real peak before 3:00 and kill the 2:00–5:24 plateau.** Turn *the map* into a 4 with a concrete, physical
   reveal (the attackers left June with an org chart, shown as one: names, roles, who signs) and cut the restatements
   (line 17 repeat, lines 53–55). Aim for the first 4 at ~2:30 and a second at ~4:30 (the undetected malware could earn it
   if the viewer *doesn't* already know the outcome — hold "it worked" back until the end of the beat).
2. **Stop spending the vault twist early.** Line 84 ("Binance's own hot wallet signed fifty of those slips") gives away
   lines 86–87. Explain the slip generically (82–83), ask "So did they find the keys?", then reveal *Binance's own
   wallet signed fifty* as the answer, and put the human cost (customers locked out, draining continued) in the same
   beat. Also drop the third "keys never stolen" (line 76) so Ch.4's two outcomes are really open. That makes it the 5
   the film lacks.
3. **Fix line 86's overstatement and the attribution gaps** in §6 (lines 1, 4, 9, 26, 59, 73, 74; date lines 70 and 103).
   Line 86 is the clip-able climax and currently states Binance's claim as fact.
4. **Rewrite the sponsor return (line 38)** as a new gap at ≥3, not a recap: start on something the viewer hasn't heard
   (e.g. the folder arriving, or "The last thing the fake company sent was a folder. It had one instruction: run it.").
   Drop "It's just a story."
5. **Make the ending bigger than the hook and close one/open one.** Signal after *twenty minutes* that the "how" isn't
   finished (e.g. line 95 → "But that's how the money left. It's not how they got in."), compress Ch.5's number pile
   (lines 102–106) to one figure, and add a question to every closing beat that currently opens nothing (line 73 → "So how
   did this one get through?"). Consider letting the payoff land on a fact rather than only the metaphor, e.g. tie the
   permission-slip reading to the Socket campaign aimed at ordinary developers, so the final turn widens to the viewer.

Smaller: cut or pay off the A.I.-tool aside (57–58); unify L5's wording; spell "pie-env" so it is said as "py-env" and
caption `pyenv`; reduce the four-line attribution stack (69–72) to two by merging the F.B.I. and T.R.M. lines.
