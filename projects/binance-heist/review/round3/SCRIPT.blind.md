**Structure:** the countdown (`script-patterns.md` §1.4). One laptop followed through five months, with an on-screen clock (T–5 MONTHS … T–0 … T+5 DAYS) on every chapter card. Runner-up: the investigation; it lost because the "who" is already named by the FBI, so the open question is "how", which a clock tells best.

**Look:** night mode (style bible). Work light on a desk for everything human; fluorescent green-black for the inside of Binance's systems; red only for annotation.

**Length:** see the critic run in LOOP.md (150 wpm, VO only). The sponsor read adds 45–75 s on top.

**Re-hooks:** `[↺]` marks one; target one every 30–40 s.

**Sponsor:** one slot after the Ch.2 reveal, about 3:00, marked `[SPONSOR SLOT — offer TBD]`. No copy yet. The first line after it opens a new question (can the fake company get past the security that caught them in June?), because it is where skippers land.

**Fact rules:**
- Every fact is in SOURCES.md (IDs in the fact map at the bottom).
- ⏱ = everything. This is a six-day-old story; re-check every number on publish day. Every status that can change carries an "as of" date in the VO.
- Binance's account is a company claim: "Binance says", "according to Binance's report", "on Binance's account". Every sentence that states a step of the attack carries it, or follows one in the same beat.
- North Korea is an F.B.I. attribution plus analysts' assessments, denied by North Korea. No one is charged. Never "North Korea stole", and never a line that puts this theft in a North Korean series as settled ("Whoever was behind this one").
- The engineers are private people: only Binance's own role labels, no faces, no blame. The June engineer is never said to have made a mistake.
- Engineer B's team: the report's label is "Software Engineer B", while A and C are labelled "Wallet …". The VO only ever says what the label says ("no 'wallet' in the title"), never that B was on or off a team.
- The "permission slip" reading of the take-home test is our interpretation and is spoken as one ("Here's how we read it").
- `[ON SCREEN: …]` lines are picture notes for shot-design, not narration.

**Markup:** `*word*` stressed · `> ` slower line · `|` beat · `[↺]` re-hook. Beat annotations follow `docs/research/emotional-engine.md` §7. Setups use `P1…` (plant IDs) so they don't clash with source IDs `S01…`.
