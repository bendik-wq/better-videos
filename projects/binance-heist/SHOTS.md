## The look
- **Night mode** throughout (style bible §Rules). Darkness is the default; light is the event. No bright-day sequences (the story never goes outdoors in daylight).
- Three light languages, one per world, so the viewer always knows where they are:
  - **Human world (DESK, DOC, FLASH):** *work light* `#fff0dc` on pure black: a desk lamp, paper under a lamp, a direct flash for evidence stills.
  - **Inside Binance's machines (VAULT, CHAINS):** *fluorescent* `#58ffa0` on green-black: single tubes over the vault, the signing machine, the slips.
  - **The world/geo (GLOBE):** night globe, sodium `#ffa860` city lights. North Korea gets **no red** (red is evidence only; a red country would read as a verdict).
- Red `#e0241b` only as annotation: the loop on the folder (0:08, callback at 12:11), "posing as", "not detected", the wallet-team box (2:27 → 5:46 callback), the one slip that works, the $6.3B bar, the apology line. ≤1 per shot. Red points only at tier 1–3 records or our labelled math (fact-check red-line rule): the "not detected" loop needs S15 verified in P1 first.
- Type: Instrument Serif for numbers and quotes, IBM Plex Mono caps for sources/dates/role labels, Archivo Narrow Bold red numerals for the countdown cards (T–5 MONTHS … T+5 DAYS) and counters.
- Finish: halation 18 @ 0.5, bloom 0.16, grain 9, vignette 0.55.
- **No realistic CG humans, no faces.** The engineers, recruiter, "CEO" and H.R. are camera-off tiles, initials, empty chairs and role labels. Every screen UI is invented and generic (no LinkedIn/Binance trademarks; the Binance name appears only as type and in documents).
- Every reconstruction (terminal, `pyenv` swap, deploy script, call screens) carries `RECONSTRUCTION` in Plex Mono, corner. Document cards are "Recreated excerpt" with verbatim text.

**Metaphors (keep once chosen):** hot wallet = a small safe with a slot · signing service = a notary stamp machine · EIP-7702 delegation = a stamped permission slip · "the vault signed for them" = a vault door opening from the inside (`visual-metaphors.md` #394, `REV-019`/REV) · cold storage = rows of sealed safes · laundering = lights splitting into thousands of paths (#112, `DAT-005` family) · the chain of trust = lit objects in a row (`DAT-011` family).
