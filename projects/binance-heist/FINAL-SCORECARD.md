## What the loop learned (feeds `studio/judge-changes.md`)
- The writer's own emotion tags were 1 point higher than a cold viewer's on most peaks, in every round. Self-scoring is worthless; blind scoring works.
- The structure improved a lot (13 → 66): spoilers removed, a reversal planted, two-outcome chapter openings, a tighter ending.
- It plateaued at 66. The judges never felt a peak before about 5 minutes. With breaking news, the first half is constrained by what is verified. Either the topic caps the peaks, or the judge is too harsh for news.
- **Real retention numbers will settle this.** Until then, treat a checklist of 66 as "good but not a breakout".
