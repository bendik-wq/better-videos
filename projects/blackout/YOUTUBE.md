## Description

> On 23 December 2015, an operator at a Ukrainian power company watched the cursor on his screen move by itself and switch off the electricity, one breaker at a time. It was the first publicly confirmed blackout caused by hackers. This is how it was done, who US prosecutors say did it, and why US agencies warn that other state hackers are already positioned inside critical infrastructure.
