Can I buy out a seller who keeps a stake? Video · 2:30

Yes. When a seller rolls over equity you can build in an early exit: a sale option (for example after three years, at a fixed price or a fixed multiple), or they ride it out and exit when you do. Options can trigger on time or on the size of the business.

How do I stop minority shareholders taking control? Video · 1:19

In the shareholders' agreement: drag-along rights, and no voting control or veto rights for minority holders. Set up the agreement, charter and constitution properly from the start. Get your own legal advice on the wording.
