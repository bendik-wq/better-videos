# The Most Dangerous Company in the World: packaging

## Angle
This uses the "mystery object" frame plus a villain/contrarian frame.

- **The object:** a palantír, the seeing stone from *The Lord of the Rings*.
- **The twist:** in the book, the stones carry a curse. Whoever looks into one can also be watched and deceived by whoever holds the master stone.
- **The company:** in 2003 a group of PayPal founders named a surveillance-software company after it. Today it sits inside the CIA, the Pentagon, ICE, the NHS and the most powerful AI deployments on earth, and it is worth more than most banks.

The central question is: **who is looking through the stone, and who is looking back?**
