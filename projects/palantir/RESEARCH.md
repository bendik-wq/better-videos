# The Most Dangerous Company in the World: research and fact-check

Compiled 2026-10-10. Every claim used in narration needs a row here. Confidence levels:
**High** means a primary document (SEC filing, court record, government release, the book itself) or several strong outlets agree.
**Medium** means one strong outlet or several secondary outlets.
**Low** means contested, a single weak source, or only paraphrased.

**Defamation rule for the script:** attribute every allegation to the person or outlet that made it ("according to The Verge", "the authors' lawsuit alleged", "Palantir denies this"). Never state an allegation as fact.

## Do not claim

- **That Palantir found Osama bin Laden.** This is a persistent rumour and has never been confirmed. If you use it, use it only as a labelled rumour.
- **That Palantir "built a database of every American"** as fact. The NYT reported expanded data-integration work, senators alleged a "mega-database", and Palantir denies building any "master list". Present all three positions with attribution.
- **That Palantir "decides who gets killed".** Maven identifies and prioritises targets for human operators. Karp's "most of the targeting in Ukraine" is his own claim. Say "helps the military find targets".
- **ELITE and ImmigrationOS operational details** beyond what 404 Media, Fortune and the contract records say. Attribute them.
- **The Gandalf quote "persuaded from afar"** about Saruman. It is not verified verbatim. Paraphrase instead ("Saruman looked into the stone, and the stone looked back").
- **Exact In-Q-Tel and Thiel seed amounts as precise numbers.** Say "about $2 million from the CIA's venture arm".
- **A precise peak market cap.** Trackers differ by $30B+ depending on the share basis. Use "about half a trillion dollars" as of Oct 2026 and date it on screen. The packaging title "$400 Billion Company" is outdated; use "$500 billion" with a date, or avoid a number.
- **Peer valuation multiples** (e.g. "10× more expensive than Microsoft"). They were not sourced in this pass.
- **That Karp "dumped" stock to cash out.** The sales were under pre-scheduled 10b5-1 plans and partly for tax withholding. State the amounts neutrally.
- **That Palantir sells or owns customer data.** Its own position (S-1) is that it "turned down opportunities to sell, collect, or mine data". Critics' concern is access and aggregation. Frame it that way.
- **The $244M Aug 2026 Pentagon memo** (single weak source).
- **That Palantir moved to Miami "because of protests".** Palantir gave no reason. Say that the move "followed protests".
- **Claude/Anthropic "supply chain risk" in connection with Maven.** It was seen only in an aggregator and is unverified.
